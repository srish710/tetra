# TETRA engineering design decisions

## Gripper — in evaluation
Single SG90 actuating two jaws. Compare guided parallel jaws and pivoting claws.
Include soft pads and mechanical stops. Measure cube mass, grip force, repeatability.

## Lift — in evaluation
Compare a servo-actuated four-bar parallelogram with a guided cable-winch elevator.
SG90 range and torque require validation under gripper + cube load.
Test a counterbalance if needed. Do not assume a large spool increases torque.

## Drive — planned
Two TT motors, L298N and a passive third support.
Test motor speed, chassis stability and raised-load tipping.
