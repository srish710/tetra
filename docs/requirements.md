# Competition requirements

From the HATCHLINGS challenge brief:
- 2:30 match.
- One 2.5 in × 2.5 in × 2.5 in cube at a time outside the Team Zone.
- Collect cubes from the Cube Location and return them to the Team Zone.
- One point per block in the zone; additional highest-stack bonus of 10 points per level above one.
- Sensor data and assembly choices may offer extra points.
- Do not interfere with opposing team in the No Contact Zone or enter opponent Team Zone.

Reconfirm the latest rules with competition organizers.
